## What it is

A free planning tool for anyone thinking about a sabbatical: a guided interview that ends in a one-page brief. It separates what to plan (money, dates, re-entry) from what to leave loose.

Everything stays in your browser. There is no account, no server and no analytics. The brief can be saved as a PDF or copied as text.

## Why I built it

As I’ve come back from being really disconnected and talked to people I worked with or alongside, I caught up with them and told them about my experience these past few months, and I heard it over and over: *I wish I could do what you’ve done. I daydream about that much time off.*

A year ago, I was in that same place - I wished I could take a bunch of time off too. And now, I have. So I’ve tried to take what I’ve learned and build something that might make the undecided (or the forced-into- it works for layoffs too) take a concrete step or two. Maybe go all the way to taking their daydream seriously.

The full story is in [What I did on my summer vacation](/pov-sabbatical/).

## What I learned

### Two lessons built into it

**Wherever you go, there you are.** Removing the demands of a full-time job did not turn me into someone with a different set of habits. I’m still me.

**I did it once; I can do it again.** Stepping away and seeing that everything was okay changed how I think about what comes next. I don’t need to find the perfect job or commit to something forever.
