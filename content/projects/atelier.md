## What it is
Atelier is a personal archive for the art you own. Each piece gets a record: what it is, where it hangs, what you paid, when you got it and what shape it is in. Around the collection sit notes on the exhibitions you have visited and a notebook for your own thinking.

## Where it goes next
The Discover page turns what you collect into what to look for next: venues worth returning to, and searches for artists and exhibitions near you.
