## What it is

Obviously, my pets are the cutest in the world (as everyone with pets says). And mine are main characters, too.

So let’s make them into a book?

Bedtime Gorilla (or Sleepytime Gorilla, as we call the persona my husband adopts to corral them at night) is one of my writing experiments, with a dollop of AI image generation on top.

## How it went

The results were… ok. With more work, I think this could become a set of cute characters with many more nighttime adventures ahead.

Not sharing the full book here, but if you think this is a good start, let me know, and maybe I'll spend some more time developing it in the future!
