You've identified a partner, or a small group of partners, and agreed to test something together. Before you begin, you need clarity on what the pilot should demonstrate, how it will run, and what evidence will support the next decision.

Pilot Design & Evaluation helps you scope a focused pilot, equip your staff to manage the partner experience, and assess results throughout the engagement. The work connects pilot activities to clear learning objectives and practical decisions about what happens next.
