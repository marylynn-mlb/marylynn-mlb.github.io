---
title: Traction Sprint
slug: traction-sprint
order: 1
row: engine
verb: Focus
color: teal
page: true

tagline: Bring commercial focus to your early partner relationships.
pitch: Prioritize partner relationships and define their potential value.
when: You have an initial collection of partner relationships and need to determine where to focus.
leave: A prioritized portfolio, initial commercial playbooks, and a 90-day action plan.
card_meta: "Fixed-fee project; typically 4–6 weeks."

best_for: Early-stage companies and new initiatives where a founder or senior leader owns partnerships, relationships have developed opportunistically, and the team needs a clearer focus. You may be pre-product, preparing to launch, or already working with early partners.

included:
  - title: A clear picture of your partner portfolio
    text: Map existing and prospective relationships by purpose, maturity, potential value, commitments, and internal effort. Distinguish commercial opportunities from relationships supporting product development, learning, distribution, credibility, or other strategic objectives.
  - title: A practical way to qualify and prioritize
    text: "Define what makes a relationship worth pursuing and what evidence would demonstrate its value. Recommend a path for each relationship: activate, test, nurture, pause, or exit. Each comes with an owner, a next step, and consideration of existing commitments."
  - title: Initial commercial partner playbooks
    text: For the agreed priority commercial partner types, define qualification criteria, the value proposition for both parties, mutual commitments, the potential path to revenue, and the next validation steps. These playbooks give your team a consistent starting point to test and refine.
  - title: Tools for managing the portfolio
    text: Create a partner tracker, clarify internal ownership and decision-making, and establish a review cadence. Define initial measures of progress and identify the supporting materials your team needs.
  - title: A focused 90-day action plan
    text: Sequence the work needed to validate assumptions, advance priority relationships, and review results. Include recommendations for ownership and staffing as the partnership function develops.

how:
  - title: Map & assess
    text: Review the portfolio, relationship history, existing commitments, and business objectives.
  - title: Prioritize & validate
    text: Assess the value each relationship could create, identify gaps, and define the next decision or test.
  - title: Build & equip
    text: Develop the initial commercial playbooks, management tools, and action plan, then walk your team through how to use them.

format_line: "Fixed-fee project; typically 4–6 weeks."
format_note: Final scope, timing, and investment depend on the size of the portfolio and number of commercial playbooks.

working_together:
  - Your team provides access to relationship history, relevant materials, and key stakeholders, and owns partner conversations and execution.
  - All agreed relationships are assessed for their potential value. Detailed playbook development focuses on selected commercial partner types; you can add playbooks for design, ecosystem, or other strategic partnerships separately.

result: A clear purpose and next step for your early partner relationships, with a practical commercial approach your team can begin to run beyond the founder.
---
Traction Sprint helps you assess that initial portfolio, manage existing commitments, and prioritize the relationships with the strongest potential. For selected commercial partner types, it establishes an initial approach to qualification, value creation, and revenue.
