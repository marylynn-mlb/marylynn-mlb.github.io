<div class="intro-copy">
<p>Most growth teams have more promising directions than capacity: a new market, a product extension, a channel, a partnership.</p>
<p>Choosing well means comparing them fairly, learning what is missing, and testing the assumptions that matter before the budget is committed.</p>
<p class="strong">Each engagement answers one question. Start with the one that matches the decision in front of you.</p>
</div>
<ol class="intro-qs">
<li><span>Which opportunities deserve attention or investment?</span></li>
<li><span>What do we need to understand about this opportunity?</span></li>
<li><span>What happens when we try a specific approach?</span></li>
<li><span>Does the broader approach work over time in a real operating relationship?</span></li>
</ol>
